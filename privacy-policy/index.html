Privacy Policy

Rune Mage: The Emberbound Grimoire (the "Game") for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S
Effective date: 29 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short
  • The Game uses your headset's passthrough camera view, your microphone, your hand tracking and a scan of your room.
  • Passthrough is shown to you on the headset so the game appears in your real room. The Game does not read, save or upload camera images or video.
  • Your voice is sent to a speech service (Wit.ai, operated by Meta) to turn it into text. The Game does not keep recordings.
  • Hand, head and room data stay on your headset. Your progress is saved only on your headset.
  • The Game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

Rune Mage: The Emberbound Grimoire is a voice and hand controlled mixed reality spellcasting game for Meta Quest headsets, developed by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). We are the developer of the Game and decide how the information described here is used by the Game. See Contact to reach us.

The Game runs on Meta Quest. Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates ("Meta") operate the headset, the Horizon OS, the Meta Quest Store and their own services, and handle information under their own privacy terms. This policy covers only what our Game does.

2. What the Game uses

The Game asks your headset for a few permissions so it can play. This table lists everything the Game can access and what happens to it.

InformationWhy the Game needs itWhere it goes
Hand tracking
positions of your hands and fingers		 You cast spells by drawing in the air, pinching and pointing, and you press menu buttons with your fingers. The Game plays with no controllers. Processed live on your headset. Not stored and not sent anywhere.
Microphone audio
your voice, including anything said near the headset while the Game is listening		 You can say spell names and commands such as "learn fire magic" or "open grimoire", and answer characters. Streamed while the Game is listening to the Wit.ai speech service (operated by Meta) through the Meta Voice SDK, which returns the words you said as text. The microphone is active while the Game is listening, so other speech or sounds nearby can also be picked up and sent. The Game keeps only the text it needs to run the command and does not store audio. See Sharing and third parties. If you decline the microphone permission, you can still play with your hands, but voice commands will not work.
Room and space data (Scene)
the shape of your room: floor, ceiling, walls, doors, windows, furniture		 So the Game can place the world, characters, trees and enemies in your real room, keep them off your furniture, and show you the scan. Created and stored by Meta's Space Setup on your headset. The Game reads it to place content while it runs. It is not copied, uploaded or saved by the Game.
Passthrough
the live view of your room		 So you can see your room with the game on top of it. Passthrough is switched on while you play and is drawn by the headset's system (Horizon OS) behind the game. The Game does not read, save or upload the camera images or any video of your surroundings. Note that your headset's own tools (screenshots, video recording, casting, Meta's Horizon features) are separate from the Game: if you use them, they can capture what you see, including your room and the Game on top of it, under Meta's terms and your settings.
Head position and movement To keep menus at eye level, aim spells and track where you walk during the room scan. Processed live on your headset. Not stored and not sent anywhere.
Game progress
spells learned, quests, achievements, settings such as voice over		 So you can continue your adventure next time. Saved in a small file on your headset only. It contains no name, account or contact details.
Text for character voices If voice over is on, the Game can read character dialogue aloud. The line of game dialogue (not your personal information) may be sent to the Meta Voice SDK text to speech service to produce audio.

What the Game does not collect. The Game does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details, does not read your Meta account profile, and does not use advertising identifiers, analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

3. How the Game uses it

  • To run the Game: recognise your gestures, understand your voice commands, place the game in your room and save your progress.
  • We do not use any of this information for advertising, profiling, tracking you across apps, or training machine learning models.
  • We do not build a profile of you and we cannot identify you from the Game.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only third party service that receives data from the Game is the speech service used for voice features:

  • Wit.ai (Meta Platforms, Inc.), through the Meta Voice SDK. When the Game is listening, short pieces of microphone audio are sent over an encrypted connection so they can be converted to text (and, if voice over is on, so dialogue can be converted to speech). Wit.ai handles that data under its own terms and Meta's privacy policy: meta.com/legal/privacy-policy and wit.ai/terms.
  • If the Voice SDK is not available on your device, the Game may use the speech recognition service built into the Android system on your headset instead, under the terms of that system component.
  • Meta. Meta provides the headset, the Horizon OS permissions (microphone, hand tracking, spatial data), the Space Setup tool and the store. Meta may collect information about your use of its platform and store as described in its own privacy policy. We do not control that and receive no personal information from it.

We may disclose information only if the law requires it, but since the Game does not collect personal information on any server of ours, we hold nothing to disclose.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

  • On your headset: your progress file stays on your headset until you delete it.
  • Voice audio: the Game does not keep it. Any retention of audio by Wit.ai is governed by Wit.ai's and Meta's terms linked above.
  • Room data: managed by Meta's Space Setup. You can delete it in your headset settings (Settings → Environment setup / Space Setup).
  • Deleting your Game data: uninstall the Game (or clear its storage in Settings → Storage). This removes your progress file. We keep no copy.

6. Your choices and controls

  • Microphone: allow or deny it when asked, or change it later under the Game's permissions in your headset's settings. The Game remains playable with hands only.
  • Room scanning and spatial data: allow or deny the spatial data permission, or manage and delete your scanned room in your headset settings. Without it, the Game uses a virtual room.
  • Hand tracking: turn it off in your headset settings; the Game needs hands to be played.
  • Voice over: can be switched off in the Game's options.

7. Children

The Game is intended for people aged 13 and over, in line with the minimum age Meta requires for Meta Quest accounts, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data security

We protect the information the Game handles with measures that fit what it does:

  • Keeping data on your device. Hand, head and room information is used live on your headset and is not sent to us or anyone else.
  • Encryption in transit. Audio and text sent for voice features travel over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection.
  • Collecting as little as possible. The Game has no accounts and holds no names, emails or other identifying details, and it does not store voice recordings or camera images.
  • Protected storage. Your progress file lives in the Game's private storage on the headset, which other apps cannot normally read, and it contains no personal details.
  • Access to services. The credentials the Game uses to reach the speech service are kept out of public code and can be replaced if needed.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Because we hold no personal information on servers of our own, there is no account database of ours that could be breached. If we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Because we do not collect personal information that identifies you, we usually have nothing to look up, but you are welcome to contact us with any request or question and we will respond within 30 days.
